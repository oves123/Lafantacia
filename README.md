# La Fantacia — Luxury Event Planning & Catering

Official responsive website for **La Fantacia Events & Catering**, serving Pleasanton, Dublin, San Ramon, and the Greater San Francisco Bay Area.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Editorial Luxury Design**: Tailored gold and cream typography, bespoke crests, and high-performance layouts.
- **Full Occasion & Catering Portfolios**: Interactive galleries, occasion showcases, and curated menus.
- **Client Testimonials**: Verified 5-star Google review showcases and interactive carousels.
- **Mobile First & Responsive**: Optimized touch-swipe navigation and smooth transitions across all viewports.
- **Complete Multi-Page Architecture**:
  - `index.html` — Homepage
  - `events.html` — Event Planning & Celebrations
  - `catering.html` — Bespoke Catering & Menus
  - `gallery.html` — Visual Atelier & Portfolio
  - `about.html` — Heritage, Story & Culinary Team
  - `contact.html` — Consultation & Inquiry Form
  - `privacy.html` — Privacy Policy
