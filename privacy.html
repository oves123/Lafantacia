Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

La Fantacia Events & Catering ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding information collection, usage, and safeguarding when you visit our website at lafantacia.com or submit an event or catering inquiry.

2. Information We Collect

When you reach out via our contact forms, telephone, or email, we may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, event dates, event location, guest counts, budget preferences, and specific culinary or decor requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your event management or catering inquiries.
  • Prepare customized styling proposals and culinary menus.
  • Coordinate services with your verified approval.
  • Maintain clear communication regarding event schedules and logistics.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is only shared with trusted event vendors (e.g. venues, floral wholesalers, equipment rentals) with your explicit consent to execute your celebration.
